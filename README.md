## Claude plans: free vs paid

Claude is available on a free plan and on paid plans. Plan details change over time, so check the [Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com) for current pricing and limits.

### Free plan

1. Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) in a web browser.
2. Enter your email address, or sign in with Google if offered.
3. Open the login link sent to your email (or complete the Google prompts).
4. Start chatting. No subscription or payment details are needed.

The free plan is available in supported locations and stays available without a subscription.

### Paid plans

| Plan | Price (US, monthly) | Who it's for | Highlights |
|------|---------------------|--------------|------------|
| Pro | $20 | Individuals | More usage per session than Free, priority access at busy times, early access to new features, Claude Code access |
| Max 5x | $100 | Heavy individual use | 5x Pro's per-session usage, priority access to new models and features, Claude Code access, monthly API credits |
| Max 20x | $200 | Heaviest individual use | 20x Pro's per-session usage, plus everything in Max 5x |

Team and Enterprise plans are also available for organizations. See the Help Center for details.

Paid plans have a session limit that resets every five hours and a weekly limit across all models. Anthropic may apply other caps. Prices vary by region, and mobile app store prices may differ.

### Upgrading from Free to Pro

1. Log in to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai).
2. Click your initials or name in the lower-left corner and open **Settings**.
3. Go to **Billing** and select the upgrade option.
4. On the upgrade page, select **Get Pro plan**.
5. Choose monthly or annual billing.
6. Enter your payment details and confirm.

### Upgrading to Max (from Free or Pro)

1. Log in to your Claude account.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Billing**.
3. Click **Upgrade to Max**.
4. Pick a usage tier: 5x or 20x.
5. Add your payment details (Free users only; Pro users already have them on file).
6. Confirm the subscription.

Moving from Pro to Max takes effect immediately, and your billing cycle restarts that day with a prorated credit for unused Pro time.

### Notes

- Paid plans require you to be in a supported location, with a phone number from a supported location on your account.
- Subscriptions renew automatically by default.
- API usage through the Claude Console is separate and is not included in Pro or Max.

## ChatGPT plans: free vs paid (2026)

ChatGPT is developed by OpenAI and is available on a free plan and several paid plans.

### Free plan

The free plan is available indefinitely with no time limit and includes:

- Access to **GPT-5.3 Instant** as the primary model, with limited/throttled access to GPT-5.5
- Basic web search (limited)
- Image generation (slower and more limited than paid plans)
- File uploads
- Access to GPTs created by others (creating custom GPTs requires a paid plan)
- ~10 messages every 5 hours on the best model before being switched to a lighter model

Note: As of 2026, the free tier includes ads.

### Paid plans

| Plan | Price (US, monthly) | Who it's for | Highlights |
|------|---------------------|--------------|------------|
| Go | $8 | Light paid users | Expanded access at the lowest paid price, includes ads |
| Plus | $20 | Individual power users | Full GPT-5.5 access, higher usage limits, priority access, custom GPT creation |
| Pro | $100–$200 | Heaviest individual use | Highest usage limits, priority access to newest models, advanced features |
| Business | $25–$30/user | Teams | Collaboration features, enhanced security and privacy |
| Enterprise | Custom | Large organizations | Enterprise-grade security, compliance, custom deployment |

### Key differences: Free vs Plus

| Feature | Free | Plus ($20/mo) |
|---------|------|---------------|
| Primary model | GPT-5.3 Instant | GPT-5.5 (full access) |
| Usage limits | ~10 msgs / 5 hrs on best model | Higher limits, consistent access |
| Image generation | Limited, slower | Faster, more generations |
| Custom GPT creation | ❌ | ✅ |
| Web search | Limited | Full |
| Priority access | ❌ | ✅ |
| Ads | Yes (as of 2026) | No |
| API access | ❌ | ❌ (separate API pricing) |

### When to upgrade

Consider upgrading from Free to Plus if you:
- Frequently hit the message limits or encounter slower responses
- Need to create custom GPTs
- Rely on ChatGPT for work and need consistent, priority access
- Need faster image generation

The free plan is sufficient for casual use, basic drafting, and occasional questions.

### Notes

- ChatGPT API access is priced separately on a pay-per-token basis and is not included in any subscription plan.
- Prices may vary by region and mobile app store prices may differ.
- Subscriptions renew automatically.

### Sources

- [OpenAI ChatGPT Pricing](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing/)
- [ChatGPT Free vs Paid Plans (2026)](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-what-is-free-chatgpt-2026/)

## Gemini plans: free vs paid (2026)

Google Gemini is available as a consumer AI assistant with a free tier and several paid plans under the **Google AI** subscription family.

### Free plan

The free plan includes:

- Access to **Gemini** (base model) for chat
- **Deep Research** (limited)
- **Gemini Live** (voice conversations)
- **Canvas** (collaborative writing/coding workspace)
- 15 GB Google storage included

No payment details are required to get started.

### Paid plans

| Plan | Price (US, monthly) | Who it's for | Highlights |
|------|---------------------|--------------|------------|
| Google AI Plus | $4.99 | Casual paid users | Gemini 3.1 Pro (limited access), video generation, NotebookLM + Audio Overviews, Gemini in Gmail & Docs, 400 GB storage, ~2× free usage |
| Google AI Pro | $19.99 | Individual power users | Full Gemini 3.1 Pro, Deep Research, Gems, Canvas, Jules, Gemini CLI, 1M-token context, 5 TB storage |
| Google AI Ultra ($100) | $99.99 | Heavy users | Everything in Pro + Deep Think, Gemini Spark, YouTube Premium Lite, ~5× AI Pro usage, 20 TB storage |
| Google AI Ultra ($200) | $199.99 | Highest compute needs | Everything in $100 Ultra + Project Genie, Mariner, up to 20× AI Pro usage, 20 TB storage (30 TB option) |

### Key differences: Free vs Pro

| Feature | Free | Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo) |
|---------|------|--------------------------|
| Primary model | Gemini base | Gemini 3.1 Pro (full access) |
| Context window | Standard | 1 million tokens |
| Deep Research | Limited | Full access |
| Gems (custom AI experts) | ❌ | ✅ |
| Gemini CLI | ❌ | ✅ |
| Jules (AI coding agent) | ❌ | ✅ |
| Video generation | ❌ | ✅ |
| Gemini in Gmail & Docs | ❌ | ✅ |
| Google storage | 15 GB | 5 TB |
| Usage limits | Free tier limits | Higher limits |

### When to upgrade to the paid plan

Consider upgrading from Free to Google AI Pro if you:
- Need the most capable Gemini model (Gemini 3.1 Pro) for complex tasks
- Require a large context window (1M tokens) for long documents or codebases
- Use Gemini within Gmail, Google Docs, or other Workspace apps
- Want access to agentic tools like Jules (coding) or Gemini CLI
- Need substantially more Google storage

The free plan is sufficient for everyday chat, basic research, and casual use.

### Notes

- The **Gemini API** is billed separately on a pay-per-token basis and is not included in any consumer subscription plan.
- Plans are available month-to-month or annually; cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period.
- Prices may vary by region; mobile app store prices may differ.
- Subscriptions renew automatically.
