## Claude plans: free vs paid

Claude is available on a free plan and on paid plans. Plan details change over time, so check the [Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com) for current pricing and limits.

### Free plan

1. Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) in a web browser.
2. Enter your email address, or sign in with Google if offered.
3. Open the login link sent to your email (or complete the Google prompts).
4. Start chatting. No subscription or payment details are needed.

The free plan is available in supported locations and stays available without a subscription.

### Paid plans

| Plan | Price (US, monthly) | Who it's for | Highlights |
|------|---------------------|--------------|------------|
| Pro | $20 | Individuals | More usage per session than Free, priority access at busy times, early access to new features, Claude Code access |
| Max 5x | $100 | Heavy individual use | 5x Pro's per-session usage, priority access to new models and features, Claude Code access, monthly API credits |
| Max 20x | $200 | Heaviest individual use | 20x Pro's per-session usage, plus everything in Max 5x |

Team and Enterprise plans are also available for organizations. See the Help Center for details.

Paid plans have a session limit that resets every five hours and a weekly limit across all models. Anthropic may apply other caps. Prices vary by region, and mobile app store prices may differ.

### Upgrading from Free to Pro

1. Log in to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai).
2. Click your initials or name in the lower-left corner and open **Settings**.
3. Go to **Billing** and select the upgrade option.
4. On the upgrade page, select **Get Pro plan**.
5. Choose monthly or annual billing.
6. Enter your payment details and confirm.

### Upgrading to Max (from Free or Pro)

1. Log in to your Claude account.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Billing**.
3. Click **Upgrade to Max**.
4. Pick a usage tier: 5x or 20x.
5. Add your payment details (Free users only; Pro users already have them on file).
6. Confirm the subscription.

Moving from Pro to Max takes effect immediately, and your billing cycle restarts that day with a prorated credit for unused Pro time.

### Notes

- Paid plans require you to be in a supported location, with a phone number from a supported location on your account.
- Subscriptions renew automatically by default.
- API usage through the Claude Console is separate and is not included in Pro or Max.
